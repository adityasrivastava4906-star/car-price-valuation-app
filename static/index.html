Methodology

Built on Real Market Signals

Five regressors and two classifiers compared with 5-fold cross-validation. XGBoost won on test R²; logistic regression led on price-tier classification.

01

Feature Engineering

Car age derived from year, one-hot encoded brands (top 10 + other), fuel, transmission, and seller type — 18 features total.

02

Model Selection

Randomized search over 15 XGBoost configurations. Polynomial Ridge and baseline linear models kept as benchmarks.

03

Dual Output

Regression predicts exact price in lakhs. Classification buckets into Budget, Mid-Range, and Premium tiers via train-set quantiles.
